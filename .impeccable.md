## Design Context
### Users
People browsing a public collection of aesthetic concepts and visual culture; discover styles, read source-backed summaries, search, filter and collect references.
### Brand Personality
Modern, minimal, distinctive. User explicitly rejected classical editorial styling and red accents, and authorized creative interpretation.
### Aesthetic Direction
Contemporary visual archive: strong sans-serif typography, chalk and charcoal surfaces, restrained mint accents, graphic art studies. Avoid antique serif type, ornamental arches, sepia treatments and generic SaaS cards.
### Design Principles
- Content remains readable and source-attributed.
- Use scale and composition, not ornament, to create character.
- Keep search, categories and saved items accessible on small screens.
- Keep original art studies clearly labeled; do not imply they are historical images.
- Honor reduced motion and visible keyboard focus.
